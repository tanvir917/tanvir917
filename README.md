## 🔬 Research & Publications  

### ⚛️ Independent Researcher – Quantum Machine Learning | Melbourne *(2025 – Present)*  
- **Silicon donor quantum sensing:** built `spinsense`, a modular Python package (geometry, hyperfine, CPMG simulation, noise and inverse-solver modules) that reconstructs nuclear-spin positions from CPMG coherence-decay signals.  
- **Benchmarked three inverse methods** on 650 synthetic signals: physics-based nonlinear least squares, a **PyTorch CNN** with Monte Carlo dropout uncertainty, and a **10-qubit quantum kernel in PennyLane**; cross-checked the forward model against **QuTiP** simulation.  
- **Validated on real experimental data** (published silicon-donor dataset on Zenodo), extracting T₂ = 2.62 ± 0.81 s, consistent with the literature, and wrote verification scripts that check each reported number against the code's output.  
- **Multi-cohort EEG benchmark** (co-author, with Deakin University's Applied Artificial Intelligence Institute): curated and validated data for a six-cohort benchmark of quantum and classical kernels for depression detection, showing that single-cohort results did not reproduce across cohorts and that quantum kernels gave no advantage over matched classical baselines.  
- **Reproducible pipelines** with fixed seeds, pinned environments and permutation-based statistical testing.
